About Our Rescue

We rescue abandoned, injured, and homeless cats, giving them food, care, safety, and love until they find their forever families.

In collaboration with local animal welfare organizations in the Philippines

PAWS Philippines CARA Welfare Philippines Strays Worth Saving (SWS) Cats of BGC

At Paws of Hope Cat Rescue, we believe no cat should feel forgotten. Some arrive scared, hungry, or lonely, but with patience and kindness, they learn to trust again. By adopting or donating, you help us save more lives.
